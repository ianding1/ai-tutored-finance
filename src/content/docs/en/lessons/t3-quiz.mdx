## Formula sheet [#formulas]

**Present value and interest rates**

- Annuity (payment $C$ at the end of each of $n$ periods): $PV = \frac{C}{r}\left[1 - (1 + r)^{-n}\right]$; growing annuity (first payment $C$, growing by $g$ each period): $PV = \frac{C}{r - g}\left[1 - \left(\frac{1 + g}{1 + r}\right)^n\right]$
- Fisher equation: $1 + r = \frac{1 + i}{1 + \pi}$

**Forwards, futures and swaps**

- Cost-of-carry model: $F = S e^{(r - q)T}$
- Covered interest parity (exchange rate as the home-currency price of one unit of foreign currency): $F = S \left(\frac{1 + i_d}{1 + i_f}\right)^T$
- Minimum-variance hedge ratio: $h^* = \rho\frac{\sigma_S}{\sigma_F}$
- Swap rate ($Z_t$ is the discount factor for $t$ years): $s = \frac{1 - Z_n}{\sum_{t=1}^{n} Z_t}$

**Options**

- Put–call parity: $C - P = S - K e^{-rT}$
- Black–Scholes: $C = S\,N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2)$, $d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + \sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}}$, $d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T}$

**Funds and household investing**

- Share of final wealth after $T$ years lost to fees: $\approx 1 - e^{-fT}$
- Funded ratio $=$ assets set aside for a goal $/$ present value of the goal discounted at the risk-free rate
- Variance of a two-asset portfolio: $\sigma_p^2 = w^2\sigma_A^2 + (1 - w)^2\sigma_B^2 + 2w(1 - w)\rho\sigma_A\sigma_B$
- Sharpe ratio: $\frac{\mu - r_f}{\sigma}$; t-value of alpha: $t \approx IR\sqrt{T}$
