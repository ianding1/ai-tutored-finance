## Formula sheet [#formulas]

**Present value and interest rates**

- Annuity (payment $C$ at the end of each of $n$ periods): $PV = \frac{C}{r}\left[1 - (1 + r)^{-n}\right]$; growing perpetuity: $PV = \frac{C}{r - g}$
- Fisher equation: $1 + r = \frac{1 + i}{1 + \pi}$

**Bonds**

- Price: $P = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \frac{\text{CF}_t}{(1 + y)^t}$
- Modified duration and convexity: $\frac{\Delta P}{P} \approx -D^*\Delta y + \frac{1}{2}C(\Delta y)^2$, $D^* = \frac{D}{1 + y}$

**Stocks and company valuation**

- Gordon model: $P_0 = \frac{D_1}{r - g}$; forward P/E: $\frac{P_0}{E_1} = \frac{1 - b}{r - g}$ ($b$ is the retention ratio)
- $\text{WACC} = \frac{E}{V}r_E + \frac{D}{V}r_D(1 - t)$
- Building blocks: $E[R] \approx y + g + \pi + \Delta v$ (shareholder yield, real growth of earnings per share, inflation, valuation change)

**Risk and portfolios**

- Variance of a two-asset portfolio: $\sigma_p^2 = w^2\sigma_A^2 + (1 - w)^2\sigma_B^2 + 2w(1 - w)\rho\sigma_A\sigma_B$
- CAPM: $E[R_i] = r_f + \beta_i(E[R_M] - r_f)$, $\beta_i = \frac{\text{Cov}(R_i, R_M)}{\text{Var}(R_M)}$
- Sharpe ratio: $\frac{\mu - r_f}{\sigma}$; optimal risky share: $w^* = \frac{\mu - r_f}{\gamma\sigma^2}$
- Long-run compound growth: $g \approx \mu - \frac{\sigma^2}{2}$
- Standard error of a sample mean: $\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$; t-value of alpha: $t \approx IR\sqrt{T}$
