## Formula sheet [#formulas]

**Consumers**

- Marginal rate of substitution: $MRS = \frac{MU_x}{MU_y}$

**Intertemporal choice and uncertainty**

- Fisher equation: $1 + r = \frac{1 + i}{1 + \pi}$
- Expected utility: $E[u(W)] = \sum_i p_i\,u(W_i)$; certainty equivalent: $u(\text{CE}) = E[u(W)]$; risk premium: $E[W] - \text{CE} \approx \frac12 A(W)\,\sigma^2$
- CRRA utility (coefficient of relative risk aversion $\gamma$): proportional risk premium $\approx \frac12\gamma\sigma^2$

**Taxes**

- With a tax of $t$ per unit, the price buyers pay $P_b$ and the price sellers receive $P_s$ satisfy $P_s = P_b - t$

**Present value**

- Perpetuity: $PV = \frac{C}{r}$; growing perpetuity: $PV = \frac{C}{r - g}$ ($C$ is the payment at the end of the first period)
- $1 + \delta + \delta^2 + \cdots = \frac{1}{1 - \delta}$ ($0 < \delta < 1$)
